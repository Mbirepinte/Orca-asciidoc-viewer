== Getting started

Welcome to the *Orbital Garden*, a made-up greenhouse that floats in low orbit. Every plant here is _imaginary_, but the markup is real.

NOTE: Switch to *Preview* mode and click any block to edit its source in place.

=== Planting checklist

* Check the gravity setting
* Prepare the substrate
** Moon dust
** Compost
* Water sparingly

. Open the airlock
. Place the seedling
. Close the airlock
